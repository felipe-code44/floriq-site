Support

We usually reply within 2 business days.

Email us at support@floriqapp.com — in English or Spanish. You can also use Settings → Report a bug in the app.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Floriq → Cancel subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

I paid but Premium isn’t unlocked

In Floriq, go to Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you paid with.

I bought extra coach messages but don’t see them

They are added once Apple confirms the payment, usually within seconds. Close the coach and open it again. If they still don’t show up, email us with the date of the purchase.

The coach asks for permission. Why?

To answer you, Floriq sends your messages and a summary of your numbers to Anthropic, the company that makes the AI. We ask before sending anything. You can take it back anytime in Settings → AI coach.

How do I delete my account and data?

Settings → Delete account. It removes your account and all its data. Remember to cancel your subscription separately in the App Store.

I forgot my password

Email us from the address on your account and we’ll help you get back in.

Is the coach a real person?

No. The coach is an AI. It explains and educates, but it doesn’t give financial advice or tell you where to invest.

Is my data safe?

We never connect to your bank or see your card details, and we don’t sell your data. Read our Privacy Policy.
