Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026
The short version
  • We only collect what the app needs to work: your answers, the numbers you type in, the expenses you log and your coach messages.
  • We never connect to your bank, never see your card details and never sell your data or use it for ads.
  • Your coach messages are processed by Anthropic (Claude) to write the reply. They are not used to train AI models.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time from Settings → Delete account.

1. Who we are

Floriq (“Floriq”, “we”, “us”) is a personal-finance app operated by Felipe Zapater, based in Spain, who is the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at support@floriqapp.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Questionnaire answers: your age, income range, monthly spending range, whether you have high-interest debt and its type, how many months of expenses you have saved, whether you invest monthly, your money mindset, your main goal and your language.
  • Account details (optional): your email and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see them.
  • Profile (optional): first name, last name and date of birth.
  • Your figures (optional): savings, what a month costs you, high-interest debt and monthly income, plus the history of these figures when you update them.
  • Expenses you log: amount, category, name and date.
  • Missions you complete.
  • Coach messages: the messages you send to the AI coach and its replies, and the monthly summaries it writes for you.
  • Support messages you send us by email.

Information created when you use Floriq

  • An account identifier (also for guest accounts created before you sign up).
  • Subscription status and purchase records (your subscription and any extra coach message packs) received from Apple through RevenueCat, and how many extra messages you have left. We never receive your card or payment details — Apple handles payment.
  • Whether and when you allowed Floriq to send your data to the AI coach.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our providers to run and secure the service (for example errors and IP addresses).

Stored only on your phone

Some preferences, such as your language, reminders and tips you have dismissed, are stored only on your device.

What we do not collect

We do not connect to your bank, and we do not collect your precise location, contacts or photos. We do not use advertising trackers.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the app: calculate your Money Score, choose your missions, and show your expenses and progress (performance of our contract with you).
  • To run the AI coach and write your monthly summary, which means sending data to Anthropic (your consent, which you give in the app before your first message and can withdraw anytime in Settings → AI coach).
  • To manage your subscription and the extra messages you buy, and to restore purchases (contract).
  • To answer support requests (contract and legitimate interest).
  • To keep Floriq secure, prevent abuse (such as limiting coach messages per day) and fix problems (legitimate interest).
  • To send reminders you turn on, such as the monthly check-in or the trial reminder (your consent, which you can withdraw in your iPhone settings).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping purchase records when required.

4. The AI coach

Before your first message, Floriq asks for your permission to send your data to Anthropic. Nothing is sent until you tap “Allow and continue”. You can take it back anytime in Settings → AI coach; the coach and the monthly summary then stop working until you allow it again.

When you message the coach, we send Anthropic’s Claude API your message, your recent conversation and a summary of your situation so it can write a reply: your first name (if you added it) and age, your Money Score and its breakdown, your level and missions, your figures and how they have changed, your questionnaire answers (goal, money mindset, income and spending ranges) and this month’s spending totals by category. We do not send your last name, date of birth, email or the names of individual expenses. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models and is deleted after a limited period (generally within 30 days), unless it must be kept longer to enforce its usage policies.

Please do not share sensitive details with the coach, such as account numbers or passwords. The coach is an AI and provides general education, not financial advice.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf under their own terms and data-processing agreements:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Anthropic — the AI that writes the coach’s replies.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Apple — App Store purchases and payments.
  • Squarespace — our domain and support email forwarding.
  • Our email provider — to receive and answer support emails.
  • GitHub — hosts this website.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. International transfers

Some providers process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our database right away; copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period before they are overwritten. Purchase records may be kept by Apple and RevenueCat as the law requires. We also keep an anonymous record of the purchase codes of message packs already used, so the same purchase cannot be counted twice. Support emails are kept as long as needed to help you.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correct: you can see and edit your answers, profile and figures in the app.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account removes your account and all its data.
  • Copy of your data, objection or restriction: email us at support@floriqapp.com.
  • Withdraw consent for the AI coach: turn off Settings → AI coach.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in your iPhone settings.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you also have the right to data portability and to complain to a data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos). If you live in a US state with a privacy law, you can exercise the rights it gives you by emailing us; we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

9. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS), each account can only read its own data, and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. This website

floriqapp.com does not use cookies, analytics or ads. It is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages), which may record visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs for security. We do not track you across other apps or websites, so we do not respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals in any special way.

11. Age

Floriq is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has used Floriq, contact us and we will delete the data.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
